# Shot list — "Why coffee should start at 8:30"

15.0s · 1920×1080 · 60 fps · 120 BPM (beat = 0.5s, bar = 2s)

Rhythm plan: **loud → cold → busy → slow/soft → loud/dense → calm.**
Every shot hands its main object to the next one, so there are no dissolves.

| # | Time | Beats | Energy | Scale | Density |
|---|------|-------|--------|-------|---------|
| 1 | 0.00–2.00 | 1–4 | high | huge | minimal |
| 2 | 2.00–4.50 | 5–9 | medium | medium | medium |
| 3 | 4.50–7.50 | 10–15 | medium-fast | small objects | dense |
| 4 | 7.50–10.00 | 16–20 | slow | huge (close-up) | minimal |
| 5 | 10.00–12.50 | 21–25 | very fast | huge | dense type |
| 6 | 12.50–15.00 | 26–30 | resolving | large | balanced |

---

## Shot 1 — HOOK: "8:15" (0.00–2.00)

- **Visual**: Paper background. "8:15" in Inter Display Black, ~640px tall, sits off-centre left.
  Running header in mono: `TEAM COFFEE — DAILY` (left) / `MEMO Nº 830` (right).
- **Text**: `8:15` · support label `CURRENT COFFEE TIME`
- **Motion**:
  - 0.00 Digits punch up from a baseline mask, staggered 50ms, hero-weight spring with overshoot.
    No fade-in at all; the first frame already has ink on it.
  - 0.50 Mono label types on (UI weight).
  - 1.00 A red pen **slash** wipes diagonally across the digits in 160ms. The digits recoil 1–2° on impact.
  - 1.45 The "8", "1" and "5" drop out of frame with gravity and spin. The **colon stays**.
- **Transition**: 1.72 The lower colon dot grows into a circular iris filling the frame with espresso → shot 2.
- **Camera**: Slight push-in (1.00→1.04) over the whole shot, a 6px shake at the slash.
- **Sound**: Kick + low "thump" on 0.00; digit clicks; paper slash + snare at 1.00; whoosh into the iris at 1.50.

## Shot 2 — "too early." (2.00–4.50)

- **Visual**: Espresso background. Foam clock face left (Ø 620px) whose pivot *is* the colon dot from shot 1.
  Hands read 8:15. Right column: editorial type.
- **Text**: mono `08:15 IS` → serif italic hero `too early.` (foam) → support serif `for those who sleep in.` (crema)
- **Motion**:
  - 2.00 Clock ticks and hands spring out from the pivot (cards weight).
  - 2.25 Hero words rise through a line mask, one per word.
  - 3.00 A **signal-red wedge** sweeps from :15 to :30 on the clock. Mono callout `+15 MIN` snaps in on the arc.
  - 3.50 Support line.
- **Transition**: 4.00–4.50 the camera dives *into* the clock face. The foam disk scales up until it becomes
  the paper background of shot 3.
- **Camera**: Slow drift right, then the dive (camera weight, accelerating).
- **Sound**: Clock-tick hats start; ratchet "zip" on the wedge at 3.00; deep whoosh on the dive.

## Shot 3 — The empty cups (4.50–7.50)

- **Visual**: Paper. A table line, five cups (the team) and one dashed empty seat on the right.
  A large mono clock top-right counts `08:15 → 08:30` and turns red at :30.
- **Text**: serif hero `The team sips at 8:15.` → swapped by mask to `By 8:30, it's all gone.`
- **Motion**:
  - 4.48–5.00 Cups land on the table one by one, card-weight springs with squash on landing.
  - 5.00–6.25 Coffee levels drain cup by cup; their steam stops.
  - 6.25 The headline swaps (old line slides up and out, new line slides up in).
  - 6.75 Two ears pop up behind the table in the empty seat. 7.00 The eyes peek and pupils look at the empty cups.
- **Transition**: 7.10–7.50 the camera pushes in on the cat (scale ×3, re-framed right) → shot 4.
- **Camera**: Static wide, then the push.
- **Sound**: Ceramic clinks per cup (pitched up across the row); sips; little "pop" for the ears.

## Shot 4 — The sad little cat (7.50–10.00)

- **Visual**: Close-up. The cat fills the right half. Left column type. This is the quietest shot: few elements, slow.
- **Text**: mono `LATE BIRDS` → serif `become sad` → serif italic hero `little cats.`
- **Motion**:
  - 7.75 Ears droop (heavy spring, slow).
  - 8.00 Pupils grow, catch-lights appear, the brows tilt.
  - 8.50 A tear forms, then 8.75–9.60 rolls down the cheek and drops.
  - The type arrives at 7.75 / 8.00 / 8.50, one line per beat.
- **Transition**: Hard cut on the downbeat at 10.00. It's the only hard cut, used deliberately for the punch.
- **Camera**: Very slow push (1.00→1.03).
- **Sound**: Music drops to the pad only. A falling "mew" glide at 8.00 and a tiny droplet at 9.60.

## Shot 5 — The punch: "JUST WAIT 15 MIN." (10.00–12.50)

- **Visual**: Full signal-red frame, espresso type set very large and tight. This is the only full-red frame in the film.
- **Text**: `JUST` (10.00) · `WAIT` (10.50) · `15` (11.00) · `MIN.` (11.50)
- **Motion**: Each word slams in on its beat from a different direction (down, right, up, left). It overshoots,
  and the earlier words get nudged by each impact so the block feels physical.
- **Transition**: 12.00–12.50 `JUST WAIT` and `MIN.` are thrown off-frame. `15` glides to its final position
  and **rolls to `30`** (vertical digit roll). A paper-coloured wipe climbs from the bottom and takes the frame back.
- **Camera**: Micro-kicks of scale (1.0→1.02) on every beat.
- **Sound**: Full beat. Kick + clap on every word; flip clicks on the roll.

## Shot 6 — The lockup: "Coffee at 8:30" (12.50–15.00)

- **Visual**: Paper. Centred lockup (the only centred composition). Serif italic `Coffee at` above the giant `8:30`.
  The colon is set in signal red. A **happy cat** peeks out from behind the `0`, then blinks.
  The lockup is shifted 70px left so lockup + cat balance optically.
  Footer mono: `EVERYONE SLEEPS. EVERYONE SIPS.` / `STARTING MONDAY`
- **Motion**:
  - 12.18 `8` slides in (critically damped, so it never touches the 3). 12.40 the red colon dots pop in on springs.
  - 12.75 `Coffee at` rises through a mask.
  - 13.00 Cat slides out from behind the 0 with happy ^ ^ eyes and an ear wiggle. 14.20 blink.
  - 13.50 Footer labels.
  - 13.50–15.00 Hold. A slow 1.8% camera push and the cat's breathing keep it from freezing. The poster frame (14.5s) comes from here.
- **Camera**: Settles from 1.03 to 1.00 (camera weight).
- **Sound**: Bright bell + resolved Cmaj9 at 12.50. Happy chirp at 13.00. Ring-out to silence by 15.0.
