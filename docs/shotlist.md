# Shot list — "Why coffee should start at 8:30"

30.0s · 1920×1080 · 60 fps · 120 BPM (beat = 0.5s, bar = 2s). `src/timeline.json` holds the exact cue times.

> **v2 pacing (30s).** v1 was 15s and felt rushed. v2 keeps the same six shots but gives each one roughly
> twice the screen time. Springs keep their physical speed, so the motion stays crisp. The extra time goes into
> *reading holds*: each line gets ~1–1.5s alone on screen before the next change, and the punch words land on
> every other beat instead of every beat.

Rhythm plan: **loud → cold → busy → slow/soft → loud/dense → calm.**
Every shot hands its main object to the next one, so there are no dissolves.

| # | Time | Energy | Scale | Density |
|---|------|--------|-------|---------|
| 1 | 0.0–4.0 | high | huge | minimal |
| 2 | 4.0–9.0 | medium | medium | medium |
| 3 | 9.0–15.0 | medium | small objects | dense |
| 4 | 15.0–20.0 | slow | huge (close-up) | minimal |
| 5 | 20.0–24.5 | punchy | huge | dense type |
| 6 | 24.5–30.0 | resolving | large | balanced |

## Shot 1 — HOOK: "8:15" (0.0–4.0)

- **Visual**: Paper background. "8:15" in Inter Display Black, ~640px tall, sits off-centre left.
  Running header in mono: `TEAM COFFEE — DAILY` (left) / `MEMO Nº 830` (right).
- **Text**: `8:15` · label `CURRENT COFFEE TIME` → `— FOR NOW`
- **Motion**:
  - 0.0 Digits punch up from a baseline mask, staggered 55ms, with a hero-weight spring. The first frame already has ink on it.
  - 0.75 Mono label. Then hold on the big number (slow camera push 1.00→1.035).
  - 2.0 A red pen **slash** wipes across in 160ms. The word recoils and the camera shakes. `— FOR NOW` appears in red.
  - 3.0 The "8", "1" and "5" drop out with gravity and spin. The **colon stays**.
- **Transition**: 3.4 The colon's lower dot grows into an iris that fills the frame with espresso → shot 2.
- **Sound**: Impact + digit clicks on 0.0, paper slash + clap at 2.0, falling blips at 3.0, whoosh into the iris.

## Shot 2 — "too early." (4.0–9.0)

- **Visual**: Espresso background. Paper clock face whose pivot *is* the colon dot. Hands at 8:15. A red second
  hand steps on every beat. Editorial type in the right column.
- **Text**: mono `08:15 IS` → serif italic hero `too early.` → `for those who sleep in.`
- **Motion**: 4.0 the clock springs open. 4.5 the hero words rise through line masks. 6.0 a red wedge sweeps from :15 to :30,
  with a `+15 MIN` callout. 7.0 the support line appears.
- **Transition**: 8.4–9.0 the camera dives into an empty patch of the clock face. The face becomes the paper of shot 3.
- **Sound**: Clock-tick hats start. A ratchet on the wedge. A deep whoosh on the dive.

## Shot 3 — The empty cups (9.0–15.0)

- **Visual**: Paper. A table line, five cups and a dashed empty seat marked `RESERVED · 8:30`.
  A large mono clock top-right counts `08:15 → 08:30` (10.0–12.6) and turns red at :30.
- **Text**: serif `The team sips at 8:15.` → swapped at 12.8 to `By 8:30, it's all gone.`
- **Motion**: 9.0 cups land one by one (card springs, squash on landing). 10.0 the cups drain one after another,
  each one tilting as it's sipped while its steam fades. 13.5 ears pop up at the empty seat. 14.0 the cat peeks at the empty cups.
- **Transition**: 14.5 the camera pushes in on the cat (×3.2) while the cups slide away → shot 4 (same world, no cut).
- **Sound**: Ceramic clinks, pitched up across the row. Sips. A pop for the ears.

## Shot 4 — The sad little cat (15.0–20.0)

- **Visual**: Close-up with the cat's chin on the table edge. The place card reads `RESERVED · 8:30`. This is the quietest shot.
- **Text**: 15.5 mono `LATE BIRDS` → 16.0 `become sad` → 17.0 serif italic hero `little cats.`
- **Motion**: 15.5 ears droop (heavy, slow spring). 16.0 pupils grow, the lids sag and the cat looks up. 17.0 a tear forms,
  17.5–19.0 rolls down and drops. Slow push 1.00→1.035 throughout.
- **Transition**: Hard cut on the downbeat at 20.0. This is the only hard cut.
- **Sound**: Drums stop and only the Fm6 pad remains. A falling "mew" at 16.0. A droplet at 19.0.

## Shot 5 — The punch: "JUST WAIT 15 MIN." (20.0–24.5)

- **Visual**: Full signal-red frame. This is the only full-red frame in the film.
- **Text**: `JUST` (20.0) · `WAIT` (21.0) · `15` (22.0) · `MIN.` (23.0), then a one-beat hold to read it.
- **Motion**: Each word slams in from a different direction and nudges the block. The camera kicks on each impact.
- **Transition**: 23.9–24.2 `JUST WAIT` is thrown up and `MIN.` is thrown down. `15` rolls to `30` and glides into
  the lockup while a paper wipe climbs from the bottom.
- **Sound**: Kick + clap + low hit on each word. A whoosh and flip clicks on the roll.

## Shot 6 — The lockup: "Coffee at 8:30" (24.5–30.0)

- **Visual**: Paper. The only centred composition, shifted 70px left so lockup + cat balance optically.
  Serif italic `Coffee at` sits over a giant `8:30` with a red colon. A **happy cat** peeks out from behind the `0`.
  Footer: `EVERYONE SLEEPS. EVERYONE SIPS.` / `STARTING MONDAY`.
- **Motion**: 24.2 the `8` slides in (critically damped). The red colon dots pop in. 25.0 `Coffee at`. 26.0 the cat slides out
  with an ear wiggle. 27.0 footer rule + labels. 27.9 and 29.1 blinks. A slow 1.8% push and the cat's breathing keep the hold alive.
- **Sound**: Bell + Cmaj9 at 24.5. A chirp for the cat. A relaxed outro with soft stabs and quiet ticks, ringing out to silence.
